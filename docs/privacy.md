# Debattle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-30

This Privacy Policy explains how Debattle ("we," "us") handles information when
you use the Debattle app on Reddit.

## 1. Information We Process
We process the minimum data required to operate the app, including:
- Reddit account identifiers (e.g., username, user id)
- Content you submit (riddle answers and optional share content)
- App usage signals (timestamps, app state for gameplay and scoring)

## 2. How We Use Information
We use this information to:
- Provide gameplay, scoring, and share functionality
- Maintain app state and progression
- Improve stability and detect abuse

## 3. Third-Party Services
Debattle relies on third-party services to operate (e.g., AI and storage
providers). These services process data on our behalf to deliver the app.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service and comply with
legal obligations. You can request deletion where applicable.

## 5. Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service
providers strictly to operate the app, or when required by law.

## 6. Security
We use reasonable measures to protect data. No system can be guaranteed 100%
secure.

## 7. Your Choices
You can stop using the app at any time. For questions or deletion requests,
contact us.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of Debattle means you
accept the updated policy.
